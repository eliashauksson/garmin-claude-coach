What it is

Garmin Coach is a training dashboard that runs on your own laptop. It reads your activities, sleep, heart rate variability and recovery from Garmin Connect, and shows them on one page: today's readiness, your training load, your progress toward your goal race.

Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, is the coach. From your goals and your recent training it drafts a season plan to race day and the next two weeks of sessions, and adjusts them when your training, feedback or recovery call for it. You accept or change every suggestion.

It only reads from Garmin; it never changes anything in your Garmin account. The app speaks English, German and French (Settings › General › Language).

What you need

  • A laptop or desktop with Windows 10 or 11, or Linux (for example Ubuntu), and about 1 GB of free space.
  • Your Garmin Connect email and password, and your phone or email inbox if you use two-step verification.
  • A Claude API key with some credit on it. You create it during the first start; it's explained below.

No administrator rights and no programming tools are needed. The installer brings everything the app uses into one folder of its own.

Install

Windows

  1. Download install-windows.cmd:
    Download the installer
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click install-windows.cmd.
  3. If a blue window says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't signed by a company, which is why Windows asks.
  4. A black window shows the progress. It downloads about 200 MB and takes a few minutes.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal: press Ctrl+Alt+T.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into the terminal (Ctrl+Shift+V) and press Enter: 
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eliashauksson/garmin-claude-coach/main/installer/install.sh | bash
  3. It downloads about 200 MB and takes a few minutes.

When it's done, Garmin Coach opens in your web browser, and there is a Garmin Coach icon on your desktop and in your Start menu or app menu. The app runs on your laptop; the browser is only its window.

Prefer a different way on Windows? Press Win+R, paste this line and press Enter: 
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eliashauksson/garmin-claude-coach/main/installer/install.ps1 | iex"

First start

The Dashboard shows a Getting started list. Work through it from the top; each step disappears from the list once it's done.

  1. Connect Garmin. Go to Settings › General, enter your Garmin Connect email and password, and click Log in. If Garmin sends you a code by email or in the Garmin Connect app, type it in and click Confirm. The first login can take a minute.
  2. First sync. It starts by itself after logging in and loads the last 4 weeks. Give it a few minutes.
  3. Connect Claude. This is where the coaching comes from, so it needs your own key:
    1. Go to platform.claude.com and create an account.
    2. Under Settings › Billing, add some credit. $10 is a good start; see What Claude costs.
    3. Under Settings › API keys, click Create key, give it a name like “Garmin Coach”, and copy the key. It starts with sk-ant-.
    4. Back in Garmin Coach, paste it into the Claude card in Settings › General and click Save and test.
  4. Load your full history. One click fetches every activity and health day Garmin has from before the first sync. It takes an hour or two in the background; you can keep using the app, and if you close it, it carries on next time.
  5. Tell the coach about yourself. On the Plan page, chat with Claude about your goal race and date, your target time, how many days a week you can train, past injuries and what you've done so far. Then let it draft your season plan to race day.

Coros bike computer

If you record rides on a Coros bike computer, such as the Coros Dura, the app can read those too. This is optional; skip it if everything you do is on Garmin.

  1. Go to Settings › General and click Connect Coros in the Coros card.
  2. The Coros sign-in page opens. Sign in with your Coros account and allow access.
  3. Coros sends you back to the app's Settings, and a sync starts.

From then on, every sync also fetches your new Coros rides, with heart rate, speed, power, cadence and the GPS track. Training stress, heart-rate zones and the map work just like for Garmin activities. Sleep, HRV and readiness still come from Garmin.

  • A ride is counted once. If a ride is on both Coros and Garmin, for example because you imported it into Garmin Connect, the app keeps one entry and uses the Coros ride's detailed data.
  • Coros lets the app download 50 rides a day. With a long history, the first sync brings your newest rides, and the rest come over the next days by themselves.
  • The Coros login renews itself; you don't need to sign in again. Disconnect in the Coros card removes it. The rides already loaded stay.

What Claude costs

You pay Anthropic directly, per request, from the credit you added. There is no subscription and Garmin Coach takes nothing. What it costs depends on how often plans are drafted and adjusted and which Claude model each task uses.

  • Settings › Claude & API costs lists every request with its cost, per day, per task and per model, so you always know where you stand.
  • There you can also choose a smaller, cheaper model for each task, and send requests nobody is waiting for through Anthropic's half-price batch service. That's the default.
  • When the credit runs out, the app says so and planning pauses. Your data and plans stay. Add credit on the billing page and click I've added funds, check again.

Start with a small amount, watch the cost page for a couple of weeks, and top up from there.

Every day

  • Open it with the Garmin Coach icon. If it's already running, the icon just opens it in your browser again.
  • The app keeps running in the background after you close the browser tab, and syncs with Garmin every 2 hours between 6:00 and 22:00. The Sync now button syncs right away.
  • Turn on Settings › General › Start with the computer so it starts by itself when you log in. Garmin keeps some numbers, like training status and race predictions, only for the day itself; the app can only save them on days it runs.
  • After a workout, answer How did it go? on the Dashboard. The coach uses your feedback when it adjusts the plan.
  • To stop it, use Settings › General › Quit the app.

Your data

Everything stays on your laptop, in the app's data folder. Settings › General › Data folder shows where it is and can move it, for example into a folder that is backed up.

WhatWhere it goes
Training and health dataThe data folder on your laptop. Read from Garmin, never sent back.
Garmin passwordPassed to Garmin once to log in, and not stored. Garmin returns a login that lasts about 6 months; it's kept in the .garminconnect folder in your user folder.
Coros loginYour Coros password goes only to Coros's own sign-in page. The app keeps the login Coros hands back in .config/coach/coros.json in your user folder, readable only by you. Nothing is ever written to Coros.
Claude API keyIn .config/coach/anthropic-key in your user folder, readable only by you.
What Claude seesTo plan, Claude receives your athlete description, your recent training, recovery data such as sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, and your workout feedback. They go to Anthropic through your own API key.

Backups

Each day before the first sync, the app saves a backup of your database and athlete description, and keeps the newest 14. Download backup in Settings saves one you can keep elsewhere, like on a USB stick. To go back to a backup, open Settings › General › Backup › Restore a backup and pick one from the list, or choose a downloaded file. The app first backs up how things are now, so a restore can always be undone.

When something goes wrong

A banner at the top of the page says what failed and what to do. The most common ones:

“Garmin login has expired”
This happens about every 6 months; the app warns you a week before. Go to Settings › General, log in again, and press Sync now.
“The last sync failed”
Usually Garmin is busy or the internet dropped. The next automatic sync tries again; nothing you have is lost.
“You have reached the daily limit of 50 activity FIT files” (Coros card)
Coros allows 50 ride downloads a day. The rides not loaded yet come with the next syncs, after Coros resets the limit. Nothing to do.
“The last Coros sync failed” with Connect again
The Coros login no longer works. Click Connect again and sign in on the Coros page.
“Your Claude API credits have run out”
Add credit on the billing page, then click I've added funds, check again.
“Can't reach the coach server”
The app isn't running. Open it with the Garmin Coach icon; the page reconnects by itself.
The icon does nothing (Linux)
Some desktops ask before running a new icon: right-click it and choose Allow Launching.
Still stuck?
The file app.log in the data folder says what the app was doing. Send it along when you ask for help.

Updating and removing

Updates. When a new version is out, Settings › General shows it with an Update now button. The app downloads the new version and restarts by itself in a minute or two; your data stays.

Removing it. On Windows, use Start menu › Uninstall Garmin Coach. On Linux, run ~/.local/share/garmin-coach/uninstall.sh in a terminal. Either way it asks whether to delete your training data too; the answer “no” keeps it, so you can reinstall later and pick up where you left off.
